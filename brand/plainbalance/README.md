# plainbalance identity

Product: **plainbalance**. Always lowercase, one word. Attribution: **plainbalance by Talarurus**.

The founder approved the lowercase naming and restrained typography direction. Final `wordmark.svg` / `wordmark-white.svg` and `lockup.svg` / `lockup-white.svg` use font-independent vector outlines. They use Inter Medium with −0.04em tracking; the complete lockups add a regular 14px `by Talarurus` line beneath the 40px wordmark. Keep lockups at least 200px wide for readable attribution. Editable live-text SVGs, JSON settings, the original OFL font/license and the outline generator are retained in `source/`. The application keeps accessible live text and its accepted layout; no UI redesign is part of this refinement. Do not lowercase the company name in prose.

The original independent white corner on an ink rounded square is the **founder-preferred plainbalance app icon**. Keep its existing geometry exactly: 64-unit viewBox, 14-unit tile radius, path `M24 20H44V40H38V26H24Z`, #111111 tile and #FFFFFF corner. `placeholder/` is a retained internal directory name, not its current approval status. No optical redesign or theme-dependent tile inversion is intended. Its single right-angle turn uses original rectangular geometry and monochrome ink/white. It shares restraint and flat geometry with the parent identity without copying, cropping or deriving the official interlocking pentagon. Do not call it a Talarurus logo iteration. Prefer the wordmark in product headers; the corner serves small icons and the app tile.

Minimum icon size: 16px. Keep proportions and clear margins; no gradients, shadows, fossil/creature imagery or extra decoration. Use `symbol-light.svg` on light surfaces and `symbol-dark.svg` on dark surfaces. `favicon.svg` and `app-icon.svg` use a white corner on an ink tile. Do not apply product filters or styling to corporate SVGs.

Regenerate 16–1024px PNGs with the MPL-licensed `render.swift`, passing this directory. The desktop builder uses those PNGs to construct ICNS; the Tauri icon is copied from the same source. Corporate exports in `app/public/brand/publisher/` stay unchanged, with their original separate rights and provenance.

Original product artwork and these SVG/text sources are MPL-2.0; no trademark clearance, registration or reserved final-mark status is claimed. Talarurus artwork remains governed by its exact separate permission. Archived Odaraia concepts are historical, excluded from builds/public source, and must not be reused as this identity.

## Preferred icon and archived exploration

The founder chose to retain the original over A: Stepped peek, B: Layered corners and C: Edge reveal. The earlier recommendation of B is superseded. All three alternatives and their review evidence are archived locally in `brand/plainbalance/archive/review-v1/`, excluded from active assets and the curated public-source manifest. The original app/favicon SVG geometry, PNG family and ICNS remain unchanged; only descriptive SVG titles and identity status are updated.

The local `preferred-review/` board shows the chosen tile at standard sizes on light/dark surfaces, with outlined wordmark and secondary attribution. This board is review material, excluded from the public source manifest. Final source/publication review is still required; preference for this icon does not authorize a commit, push, remote rename, website deployment or media publication. The clean website design and all official Talarurus assets remain untouched.

The unchanged Inter source font in `source/fonts/` uses **OFL-1.1**, with its original license and provenance. It is not MPL-covered corporate artwork. Product artwork and original generator/settings remain MPL-2.0; separately licensed font and publisher artwork retain their own terms.
