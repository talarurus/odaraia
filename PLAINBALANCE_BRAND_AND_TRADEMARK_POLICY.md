## Names, forks and publisher attribution

Ordinary truthful references such as “plainbalance by Talarurus,” compatibility statements, reviews and links are welcome. Do not imply that an unofficial build, altered application or commercial service is maintained, certified, signed, supported or endorsed by Talarurus. Do not use the company/product names or logos to impersonate official infrastructure, packages or downloads.

Unmodified source and clearly identified unofficial builds may accurately state their origin. Modified distributions should choose a distinguishable public name and their own publisher/app-store identity; describe the relationship, for example, “Based on plainbalance by Talarurus; unofficial build maintained by [maintainer].” Use independently owned or reusable neutral artwork. If retaining the corporate SVG under the narrow permission, place it only with accurate original-project attribution, not as the modified build's publisher identity.

Visible “by Talarurus” UI text may be replaced or removed to avoid misleading attribution; retain required source copyright/license notices. Brand rules govern use of reserved marks and separately permitted artwork, not whether someone may exercise MPL software rights. Do not demand disclosure of private modifications as a condition of descriptive reference, or forbid lawful competing software.

Package namespaces, signing identities, domains and distribution accounts controlled by Talarurus are not transferred by the software license. Forks should use their own distribution identifiers and signing credentials without pretending to be an official publisher. Stable database/backup format identifiers are interoperability details, not grounds to require a schema change merely for licensing or branding.
